The travel infrastructure for Eurovision 2027 is rapidly expanding. In response to the massive international interest, aviation authorities and major European airlines have announced the launch of new direct flight routes connecting major German hubs directly to Burgas Airport (BOJ).

## Direct Connections from Munich and Frankfurt

Starting in early May 2027, just in time for the Eurovision Semi-Finals on May 11 and 13, travelers will benefit from daily direct flights from **Munich (MUC)** and **Frankfurt (FRA)**. These two airports are among the largest transit hubs in Europe, meaning fans from across the globe, especially Western Europe and the Americas, can now reach Burgas with a single layover.

Previously, many international visitors had to fly into Sofia (SOF) or Istanbul (IST) and arrange ground transportation to the coast. While [VIP transfers from Sofia](/vip-transfers) remain a highly popular and comfortable option for delegations, these new direct flights provide a much-needed alternative for independent travelers and fan groups.

## Booking Advice for Fans

With the Grand Final scheduled for May 15, 2027, demand for these direct flights is expected to be unprecedented. Tickets are anticipated to go on sale in late 2026. We strongly advise fans to:

1. **Set price alerts** on major flight aggregators for the MUC-BOJ and FRA-BOJ routes.
2. **Book accommodation early:** The influx of direct arrivals means hotels in central Burgas will fill up faster than initially projected. Check our guide on [Where to Stay](/where-to-stay).
3. **Consider alternative airports:** If direct flights sell out, flying into Varna (VAR), Sofia (SOF), or Istanbul (IST) are excellent backup plans. Our concierge team can arrange seamless ground transportation from any of these locations.
