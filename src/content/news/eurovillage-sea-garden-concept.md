As the dust settles on the host city announcement, the focus is quickly shifting to the fan experience in Burgas. While official EBU announcements are still months away, local insiders and tourism boards are already painting a vivid picture of what Eurovision 2027 will look like.

## A Seaside Festival Concept
Unlike previous contests held in massive, landlocked capitals, Burgas is actively leaning into a **"seaside festival"** concept. The goal is to blend the high-energy Eurovision bubble with the relaxed, coastal culture of the Bulgarian Black Sea.

Because of this, the city's famous **Sea Garden (Морска градина)** is widely expected to serve as the main hub for Eurovision-related festivities, including the highly anticipated **EuroVillage**. 

## Why the Sea Garden is Perfect
Stretching for kilometers along the coastline, the Sea Garden isn't just a park—it's the cultural heart of Burgas. Its massive size, sprawling coastal paths, and existing open-air venues (like the Summer Theatre) make it the absolute perfect location to host thousands of international fans. 

If rumors are true, fans can expect daily live performances, food trucks, and sponsor activations, all set against the stunning backdrop of the Black Sea. Imagine dancing to your favorite Eurovision entries with a sea breeze and a cocktail in hand!
