Fans planning to travel overland to Burgas for the Eurovision Song Contest 2027 just got a massive piece of good news. 

For years, the Bulgarian state railways (BDZ) have relied on aging rolling stock for the cross-country journey between the capital, Sofia, and the Black Sea coast. However, that is officially changing.

## Modernizing the Journey to Burgas
Following an announcement by Minister of Transport Georgi Peev, Bulgaria has officially begun rolling out its brand-new fleet of **Alstom electric multiple-unit trains**. While the first trains will initially serve the Sofia-Plovdiv-Svilengrad corridor, the Minister explicitly confirmed that the new trains will be deployed on the long-distance route to **Burgas** in time for the summer season.

With all 35 new Alstom trains scheduled for delivery by **April 2027**, the fleet will be fully operational just weeks before the Eurovision 2027 semi-finals kick off at Arena Burgas in May.

## Why This Matters for Eurovision Fans
The journey from Sofia to Burgas by train typically takes around 6 hours. While it is incredibly scenic—passing through the Balkan mountains and the Thracian plain—the older BDZ trains lacked modern amenities. 

With the new Alstom trains, fans can expect:
*   **Modern Comfort:** Air conditioning, ergonomic seating, and quieter cabins.
*   **Connectivity:** Power outlets and modern passenger information systems.
*   **Reliability:** Brand-new rolling stock means fewer delays and a much smoother ride.
*   **Speed:** The trains are capable of reaching 160 km/h on upgraded sections of the track.

If you are flying into Sofia (SOF) and want to experience the Bulgarian countryside before arriving at the host city, the train is now a highly competitive and comfortable alternative to taking a domestic flight or the bus.
