**Short answer:** **36 countries** will compete at the Eurovision Song Contest 2027 in Burgas, one more than in 2026. **Canada makes its debut**, **North Macedonia returns** after four years, and **Spain and Slovenia** have until December 2026 to decide whether they take part. The list was confirmed by the European Broadcasting Union (EBU) on 28 October 2026.

*Last updated: 17 November 2026*

## Key facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Number of countries | 36 (Spain and Slovenia undecided) |
| Debut | Canada (CBC/Radio-Canada) |
| Return | North Macedonia (MRT), after four years |
| Withdrawals | None announced |
| Pre-qualified for the Final | Bulgaria (host), France, Germany, Italy, United Kingdom |
| Semi-final allocation draw | Burgas, January 2027 |
| Dates | Semi-finals 11 & 13 May, Grand Final 15 May 2027, Arena Burgas |

## Which countries are taking part in Eurovision 2027?

Albania, Armenia, Australia, Austria, Azerbaijan, Belgium, **Bulgaria**, **Canada**, Croatia, Cyprus, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Georgia, Germany, Greece, Israel, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Moldova, Montenegro, **North Macedonia**, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, San Marino, Serbia, Sweden, Switzerland, Ukraine and the United Kingdom.

## What about Spain and Slovenia?

RTVE (Spain) and RTVSLO (Slovenia) are not on the list yet. The EBU has said both broadcasters have not decided whether they will participate and has given them until December 2026 to confirm. We will update this page when they announce.

## How does the 2027 format work?

- Two semi-finals on **Tuesday 11 May** and **Thursday 13 May 2027**; each sends **10 songs** to the Final.
- The host (Bulgaria) and France, Germany, Italy and the UK go straight to the **Grand Final on Saturday 15 May**.
- The semi-final allocation draw takes place **in Burgas in January 2027**.

## New rules for 2027

- Artists must be at least **18 years old** on the first rehearsal day (previously 16).
- Backing tracks may not replace or unduly assist live vocals.
- The EBU can appoint an alternative host if conflict or instability affects the winning country.

## Which artists are already confirmed?

Most countries choose their act between January and March 2027 through national finals or internal selection. Early announcements include Cyprus, which revealed Giorgos Perris as its representative in early November 2026. Bulgaria's own selection has not yet been announced.

## FAQ

**How many countries are in Eurovision 2027?**
36 confirmed as of 28 October 2026, with Spain and Slovenia still to decide.

**Is Canada really in Eurovision?**
Yes. CBC/Radio-Canada became a full EBU member in July 2026 and will debut in Burgas.

**Is Russia taking part?**
No. Russia is not on the list of participating broadcasters.

**When will the running order be known?**
The semi-final allocation draw is in January 2027; the running order is usually published closer to the contest.
