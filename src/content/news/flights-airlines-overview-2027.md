Getting to Burgas (BOJ) for the Eurovision Song Contest in May 2027 requires a bit of planning. While it is the second busiest airport in Bulgaria, its schedule is highly seasonal. Fortunately, the Eurovision week aligns perfectly with the start of the European summer flight schedules.

Here is an honest breakdown of your flight options, whether you are flying from London, Madrid, or as far away as New York or Sydney.

## 1. Direct Flights to Burgas (LCCs)
If you are already in Europe, Low-Cost Carriers (LCCs) are your best bet.

*   **Wizz Air:** Maintains a major seasonal base in Burgas. In May, you can expect direct flights from cities like London (Luton), Vienna, Budapest, and several cities in Poland and Germany.
*   **Ryanair:** Usually begins its summer schedule in late April or early May. Expect direct connections from London (Stansted), Dublin, Vienna, and Bratislava.

*Insider Tip:* Low-cost airlines typically release their summer 2027 schedules around September/October 2026. Set up Google Flight alerts now, because prices will skyrocket the moment flights are officially loaded into the system.

## 2. Flying via Sofia (SOF) or Varna (VAR)
If you cannot find a direct flight to Burgas on your dates, do not panic. 

*   **Via Sofia (SOF):** Sofia is the capital and has year-round flights from almost every major European city. From Sofia, you can take a 50-minute domestic flight with **Bulgaria Air** directly to Burgas, or take a 5.5-hour comfortable coach ride for just €20.
*   **Via Varna (VAR):** Varna is just 2 hours north of Burgas by bus. It also has a strong low-cost network (Wizz Air and Ryanair) and is a great alternative gateway to the Black Sea coast.

## 3. Global Connections: The LOT Polish Airlines Strategy
What if you are flying from North America, Asia, or a smaller European city without direct flights? The smartest and most reliable route is connecting through **Warsaw (WAW)** with **LOT Polish Airlines**.

LOT operates a massive global network and offers seasonal direct flights from Warsaw to Burgas, as well as year-round daily flights to Sofia. 
*   **From the US & Canada:** LOT flies direct to Warsaw from New York (JFK), Chicago (ORD), Miami (MIA), Los Angeles (LAX), and Toronto (YYZ).
*   **From Asia:** Direct flights to Warsaw from Tokyo, Seoul, and Beijing.
*   **Seamless Connection:** You can check your bags all the way from your origin city directly to Burgas. The connection in Warsaw's Chopin Airport is generally quick and highly efficient.

Because LOT is a full-service legacy carrier (Star Alliance), you get a much more generous baggage allowance—essential if you are bringing heavy Eurovision party outfits or national flags!

## 5. Another Route Option: Air Serbia via Belgrade
If you are travelling from Southeastern Europe or connecting through the Balkans, it is worth checking **Air Serbia**, the national carrier of Serbia, as an alternative to the Warsaw route above. Air Serbia operates out of Belgrade Nikola Tesla Airport (BEG), a fast-growing regional hub with connections across Europe, the Middle East, and North America, and offers convenient onward links toward Bulgaria and the Black Sea coast.

## 6. What If Your Flight Is Delayed or Cancelled?
With so many connections, seasonal schedules, and a single Eurovision week driving huge demand, flight disruptions are worth planning for. Under EU and UK air passenger rights rules, you may be entitled to compensation of up to €600 if your flight to or from Burgas, Sofia, or Varna is delayed by several hours, cancelled, or overbooked, even on a connecting itinerary through Warsaw or Belgrade.
