**Short answer:** tickets for the Eurovision Song Contest 2027 in Burgas will go on sale in **January 2027**. Nine shows at Arena Burgas will be sold — the two semi-finals, the Grand Final and six preview shows between **10 and 15 May 2027**. To take part in the sale you must first register (free) as a **Eurofan** on the official Eurovision website. The exact sale date, prices and seat categories have **not** been announced yet.

## When do Eurovision 2027 tickets go on sale?

On 16 November 2026 the organisers confirmed via the Burgas Municipality that sales will start in **January 2027**. The European Broadcasting Union (EBU) describes the timing as "early 2027". No exact date or time has been published, and the sale is expected to be announced on the official Eurovision channels shortly before it opens.

## Which shows can I buy tickets for?

Nine shows in total will be ticketed, all at Arena Burgas:

- **Semi-final 1** — Tuesday 11 May 2027 (live, 21:00 CEST)
- **Semi-final 2** — Thursday 13 May 2027 (live, 21:00 CEST)
- **Grand Final** — Saturday 15 May 2027 (live, 21:00 CEST)
- **Six preview shows** — between Monday 10 May and Saturday 15 May 2027

At past contests, each live show has had two public dress rehearsals: an evening "jury show" the night before and a daytime "family show". BNT has not yet published the exact 2027 preview schedule, so do not book a specific day around a preview show until it is confirmed.

**Tip:** preview shows are usually cheaper and easier to get than the Grand Final, and you still see every act on stage.

## How do I buy Eurovision 2027 tickets?

1. **Create a free Eurofan account** on the official Eurovision website now. Registration is not a purchase — it is your entry pass to the sale and gives you sale alerts.
2. **Watch the official channels only** — eurovision.com, eurovision2027.bg and BNT.
3. **Be online at the sale start.** Demand for a first-ever contest in Bulgaria, in a seaside arena smaller than recent venues, is expected to be very high.

## FAQ

**Can I buy Eurovision 2027 tickets now?**
No. Official sales open in January 2027. Any site selling tickets today is not official.

**Do I need a Eurofan account?**
Yes. The EBU requires everyone who wants to buy tickets to register as a Eurofan before the sale. It is free.

**Is the Grand Final on a weekend?**
Yes — Saturday 15 May 2027, starting at 21:00 CEST (22:00 local time in Burgas).

**How many people does Arena Burgas hold?**
Arena Burgas opened in 2023 and is Bulgaria's newest sports and entertainment venue. The final seating capacity for Eurovision depends on the stage layout, which has not been published.
