Getting from Sofia to the Eurovision 2027 host city is going to be easier than ever. 

In response to massive anticipated demand from international fans, delegations, and press, the national carrier **Bulgaria Air** has officially announced plans to increase the frequency of its domestic flights between **Sofia Airport (SOF)** and **Burgas Airport (BOJ)**.

## Expanded Schedule for May 2027
Typically, the domestic route operates with a reduced off-season schedule in the spring. However, for the specific period surrounding the contest—from **May 2 to May 19, 2027**—Bulgaria Air will significantly boost its capacity. 

While the exact timetable is yet to be finalized, the airline confirmed that there will be multiple daily rotations, ensuring seamless connections for international passengers arriving in Sofia from European and intercontinental destinations.

## Why This is a Game Changer
As we've mentioned in our [Flights & Airlines Overview](/news/flights-airlines-overview-2027), flying into Sofia is often the cheapest and most flexible option for international fans, since it operates year-round as a major hub. 

With Bulgaria Air stepping up its domestic operations:
1. **Shorter Transit Times:** You won't have to wait hours at Sofia Airport for the single daily connecting flight.
2. **More Capacity:** More seats mean prices for the 50-minute hop to Burgas will remain somewhat competitive, despite the Eurovision rush.
3. **Baggage Through-Check:** As a full-service carrier, Bulgaria Air allows you to check your luggage all the way through if you are flying with partner airlines.

Between this announcement and the recent news of [brand new Alstom trains](/news/alstom-trains-sofia-burgas-2027) on the Sofia-Burgas railway line, the transport logistics for Eurovision 2027 are shaping up beautifully!
