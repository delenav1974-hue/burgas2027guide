If you needed one more reason to book your Eurovision 2027 trip to the coast instead of the capital, here it is: GetYourGuide, one of the world's biggest tour and activity platforms, just told millions of its readers to do exactly that.

## "Your Next Summer Holiday" Instead of Just a Song Contest

In a feature published in mid-August, GetYourGuide walked its international audience through the whole story: how Dara's "Bangaranga" win in Vienna handed Bulgaria the right to host, how four cities (Sofia, Burgas, Varna, and Plovdiv) competed for the honor, and how Burgas, a coastal city of around 200,000 people, beat the capital to the punch.

Their conclusion matches exactly what we've been telling readers since day one: Sofia may have the bigger airport and more hotel rooms, but only Burgas lets you turn your Eurovision trip into an actual beach holiday.

## What International Media Is Loving About Burgas

The piece singles out the same spots we send our own readers to every week:

- **The Sea Garden** - Burgas's multi-kilometer coastal park, praised as one of the best places in the city to unwind between events.
- **The Burgas Pier** - highlighted as one of the best sunset-watching spots on this stretch of the Black Sea.
- **Direct flights** from London, Rome, and Berlin, making Burgas an easy add-on for fans traveling from across Europe.
- **Arena Burgas** itself - confirmed as the venue for both Semi-Finals (May 11 and 13) and the Grand Final (May 15, 2027), matching everything we've already reported on the arena's [technical specs](/news/arena-burgas-tech-specs).
