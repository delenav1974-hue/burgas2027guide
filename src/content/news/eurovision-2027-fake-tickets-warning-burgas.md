**Short answer:** no one can legally sell Eurovision 2027 tickets yet. Official sales open in **January 2027**, and only through the official Eurovision channels and BNT. In early November 2026 both the **Burgas Municipality** and Bulgarian National Television (**BNT**) warned that websites are already offering tickets for the contest — those offers are not valid.

*Last updated: 17 November 2026*

## What happened?

On 5–6 November 2026, BNT and the Burgas Municipality publicly warned fans about misleading offers and unofficial ticket sales for the Eurovision Song Contest 2027. Sites have been found selling passes for Arena Burgas months before the official sale. The organisers say buying through such platforms carries a real risk of financial fraud and of ending up with an invalid ticket.

## Where can I buy real Eurovision 2027 tickets?

Only through official channels:

- **eurovision.com/tickets** (EBU) — you need a free **Eurofan** account
- **BNT** and the official host site **eurovision2027.bg**

Sales start in January 2027. Full details: [Eurovision 2027 tickets — what we know](/news/eurovision-2027-tickets-burgas-january/).

## How to spot a fake Eurovision ticket offer

- **It is selling now.** Before January 2027, every "ticket" is fake or speculative.
- **It quotes official 2027 prices.** Prices have not been published.
- **It asks for card details or a bank transfer to an unknown intermediary.** The organisers advise never to share personal or banking data with unverified sellers.
- **It promises "guaranteed" Grand Final seats** or "VIP packages with tickets included" from an unknown company.
- **The domain looks almost official** — extra words, odd endings, or "eurovision" mixed with "tickets", "official", "2027".
- **Pressure tactics** — countdown timers, "only 3 left", "price rises tomorrow".

## Already paid a suspicious site?

1. Contact your bank or card issuer immediately and ask for a chargeback.
2. Keep screenshots, emails and the website address.
3. Report the site to the Bulgarian police (dial 112 in an emergency) or the cybercrime unit in your own country.

## FAQ

**Are Eurovision 2027 tickets on sale on resale sites?**
No official tickets exist yet, so any resale listing today is selling something that has not been issued.

**Will travel agencies sell ticket packages?**
Any package that claims to include an Eurovision 2027 ticket before the official sale cannot guarantee it. Check the terms and the refund policy carefully.

**Is the Eurofan account the same as a ticket?**
No. Eurofan is a free registration that lets you enter the official sale and receive news. It does not reserve a seat.
