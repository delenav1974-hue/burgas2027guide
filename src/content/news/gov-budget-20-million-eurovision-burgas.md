The countdown to Eurovision 2027 is officially on, and the Bulgarian government is throwing its full weight behind making it the most spectacular contest yet. Following the selection of Burgas as the host city, the government has announced a massive **€20 million allocation** specifically dedicated to event preparation.

## Where is the €20 Million Going?

Hosting a mega-event like the Eurovision Song Contest requires flawless logistics, and local authorities are wasting no time. According to the latest reports, the budget will be strategically invested in four key areas:

1. **Security & Crowd Management:** Ensuring a safe environment for tens of thousands of international fans around Arena Burgas and the EuroVillage in the Sea Garden.
2. **Transport Infrastructure:** Upgrading local public transit, expanding shuttle services from Burgas Airport (BOJ), and coordinating with national rail services for fans arriving from Sofia and Istanbul.
3. **Accommodation Readiness:** Supporting the city's hospitality sector in scaling up to meet the unprecedented demand for beds.
4. **City Branding & Beautification:** Preparing the coastal city's public spaces to shine on global television.

## Beyond the Arena: St. Anastasia and Chengene Skele

Perhaps the most exciting news for traveling fans is the organizing committee's ambition to expand the "Eurovision Experience" far beyond the walls of Arena Burgas. 

Event planners have confirmed that the official cultural and tourist program will heavily feature two of Burgas's most iconic coastal landmarks: **St. Anastasia Island** and the traditional fishing village of **Chengene Skele**.

Fans can expect exclusive boat parties, themed cultural nights, and unique seaside events that will showcase the very best of the Bulgarian Black Sea coast.
