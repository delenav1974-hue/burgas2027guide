**Short answer:** on 16 November 2026 the Burgas Municipal Council voted to **expand and extend the 2027 construction ban** because of Eurovision. For the first time it covers residential districts — including **Zornitsa, Izgrev and Slaveykov** — during the contest period from mid-April to mid-May 2027. For fans this means quieter streets and no building sites next to your rented apartment.

*Last updated: 17 November 2026*

## Which areas are covered, and when?

According to Burgasnews (citing Sega), the council adopted the following periods for 2027:

| Area | Construction ban |
|---|---|
| Residential districts **Slaveykov, Zornitsa, Izgrev** | 15 April – 16 May 2027 (contest period only) |
| **City centre, Vazrazhdane, Bratya Miladinovi, Lazur** | 15 April – 16 May and 28 June – 29 August 2027 |
| **Sarafovo, Kraimorie, Primorski Park, Ezero Park, Iztok zone** | 15 April – 6 September 2027 |

Only urgent emergency repairs and geoprotection work are allowed.

⚠️ Other Bulgarian outlets (24 Chasa, Mediamall) reported slightly different dates from the same session — 14 April to 25 May — and added the new Madika and Horizont neighbourhoods. The final text will be published on the Burgas Municipality website; we will update this table once it is out.

## Why did Burgas do this?

Arena Burgas is surrounded by residential neighbourhoods. Council members agreed that the city should present itself as a **clean and welcoming host** and that Eurovision visitors should not be greeted by cranes and scaffolding. Bulgarian law already bans construction near the sea from 15 May to 1 October; municipalities may add stricter rules, which Burgas has now done.

Builders and investors objected: one councillor estimated the ban could affect around 6,000 people in the construction industry. The original proposal — a full ban from 15 April to 6 September everywhere — was softened before the vote.

## What does this mean for Eurovision visitors?

- **Staying in Zornitsa, Izgrev or Slaveykov** — large residential districts popular with budget travellers — should now be quiet during contest week. These areas are mostly apartment blocks, so they are a good-value alternative to central hotels.
- **Lazur and the city centre** — close to the Sea Garden, where the EuroVillage is expected — are also covered.
- **Sarafovo and Kraimorie** — seaside areas near the airport — have the longest ban, until early September.

## FAQ

**Will there be construction noise in Burgas during Eurovision 2027?**
Building work is banned in the main residential districts and the centre from mid-April to mid-May 2027, so noise should be minimal except for emergency repairs.

**Does the ban affect hotels opening?**
It applies to construction and installation work, not to hotels operating. Projects not finished by mid-April will have to pause.
