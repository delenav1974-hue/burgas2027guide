Deciding to travel solo to a massive festival like the Eurovision Song Contest can feel intimidating, but trust us—it is the best way to make friends from all over the world. During the contest, borders disappear, and if you are traveling alone, you automatically become part of a huge international family.

Burgas 2027 is the perfect city for your first solo adventure. Here are some honest tips from locals on how to make your trip soulful, safe, and unforgettable.

## 1. How safe is Burgas?
This is usually the first question solo tourists ask. Burgas is a very calm and incredibly safe city. The central streets (Bogoridi and Aleksandrovska) and the Sea Garden are perfectly lit all night long, with families and young people always out and about. You can safely walk back to your accommodation after late rehearsals or EuroClub parties. The main rule, as everywhere in Europe, is simply to keep an eye on your personal belongings in large crowds.

## 2. Where is it comfortable to eat alone?
In Bulgaria, it is completely normal to dine alone, and no one will give you a second look. If you want a bustling atmosphere and fast service, drop by **Happy Bar & Grill** right in the center—it is always full of people and offers great local and international food. If you prefer a cozy vibe with a book and coffee before a Semi-Final, head to the **Cardamon** coffee shop or **Incanto** restaurant for a nice view. Almost everywhere has menus in English.

## 3. How to get around without stress?
You won’t have to spend a fortune on taxis. The public transport system in Burgas is one of the best in the country.
* **Bus #15** is a highly convenient route that runs directly from the airport to the city center.
* A fleet of brand-new eco-buses operates throughout the city. You can pay your fare simply by tapping your contactless bank card on the terminal inside the bus (no need to worry about exchanging local currency!).
* Download the **BurgasBus** app in advance or just use Google Maps—both perfectly display routes to Arena Burgas.

## 4. Where to meet people?
If you want to find company for the evening, head straight to the **EuroVillage** (expected to be in the Sea Garden) or the EuroClub. A quick life hack: wear a t-shirt with your country's flag or merch from past Eurovision contests. It is the ultimate icebreaker! We also highly recommend joining OGAE fan groups on Facebook or thematic Telegram chats in advance, where solo tourists arrange to go to parties together.

## 5. What to do during the day alone?
The contest weeks are a marathon, and you will definitely need time to recharge.
* Treat yourself to a digital detox and visit a SPA in the neighboring town of **Pomorie**, which is famous for its healing mud baths.
* Take a short boat trip to **St. Anastasia Island** (tickets are sold right at the Port of Burgas). It is the perfect place to drink coffee with a view of the sea, far from the city noise.
* Simply rent a bicycle and ride along the endless bike path from the center of Burgas to the beautiful pink Salt Pans (Solnitsi).
