The "Eurovision effect" is already reshaping transport logistics to the Bulgarian Black Sea coast!

Typically, most international airlines do not begin their summer seasonal routes to **Burgas Airport (BOJ)** until late May or early June. However, with tens of thousands of fans, press, and delegations needing to arrive for the Eurovision Song Contest 2027 in mid-May, airlines are beginning to adjust their schedules.

## Austrian Airlines Leads the Way
**Austrian Airlines** is the first major international legacy carrier to officially react to the Eurovision 2027 host city announcement. They have confirmed that they will launch their seasonal direct flights between **Vienna (VIE) and Burgas (BOJ)** significantly earlier than usual, with the first flights taking off on **May 8, 2027**.

This is fantastic news for fans globally, as the Star Alliance hub in Vienna provides excellent, seamless connections for travelers coming from North America, Asia, and Western Europe.

## A Crucial Alternative to Warsaw
Earlier this week, we highlighted how LOT Polish Airlines via Warsaw is a prime connecting route for international fans. With Austrian Airlines moving up their schedule, fans now have a second major Star Alliance hub to choose from. 

This added capacity will be vital in keeping flight prices somewhat manageable during the peak Eurovision rush.
