With Burgas officially preparing to host the biggest music event on the continent, securing your travel logistics is the first step to a stress-free trip. If you are wondering **how to fly to Burgas 2027**, the good news is that **Burgas Airport (BOJ)** serves as a massive summer hub, connecting the Black Sea coast directly to over 50 destinations across Europe and the Middle East.

Here is your ultimate **Burgas Airport flight guide** for the upcoming season, optimized for fans looking for the best routes.

### 🇬🇧 Flying from the UK to Burgas
The UK boasts the highest number of direct connections to Burgas. If you are flying from Britain, you have multiple options:
*   **London:** Direct flights are available from three major airports—*Luton (LTN)* via Wizz Air and Ryanair, *Gatwick (LGW)* via easyJet and TUIfly, and *Stansted (STN)* via Jet2.
*   **Regional UK:** You can also catch direct flights from Manchester, Birmingham, Bristol, Liverpool, Leeds, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Newcastle, East Midlands, and Cardiff.

> **Insider SEO Tip:** When searching for **cheap flights to Burgas**, always compare the transfer costs from your local airport. A cheap flight from a distant airport might end up costing more in domestic train tickets!

### 🇩🇪 Germany to Burgas Routes
German fans have incredible connectivity to the Eurovision host city. Direct flights are available from:
*   Berlin, Dresden, Düsseldorf, Frankfurt, Hamburg, Hannover, Cologne, Leipzig, Munich, and Stuttgart.
*   **Airlines:** Discover Airlines, Eurowings, easyJet, Sundair, and TUIfly.

### 🇵🇱 Poland & Central Europe
If you are traveling from Poland, you can fly directly from Warsaw (Chopin, Modlin, and Radom), Gdansk, Katowice, Krakow, Lublin, Poznan, and Wroclaw via Ryanair, Wizz Air, LOT, and Enter Air.

Other Central European hubs include:
*   **Austria:** Vienna (Austrian Airlines, Ryanair, TUIfly) and Salzburg.
*   **Czechia:** Prague, Brno, Ostrava.
*   **Hungary:** Budapest (Ryanair, Wizz Air).

### 🌍 Scandinavia & Other Hubs
Fans from Northern Europe can utilize direct connections from Copenhagen, Helsinki, Oslo, Stockholm, Riga, and Tallinn, primarily served by Norwegian and airBaltic. There are also direct flights from Paris, Rome, Dublin, and Tel Aviv.

### The "Burgas Airport Transfers 2027" Warning
Booking your flight is only half the battle. **Burgas airport transfers 2027** will be heavily congested during the Eurovision weeks. While BOJ is conveniently located just 15 minutes away from the Arena by car, we strongly advise pre-booking your airport transfer. 

Public buses run from the airport to the city center, but they do not operate late at night. If you are staying in the northern resorts (Sunny Beach, Nessebar) or the southern coast (Sozopol), securing a private transfer or a rental car is mandatory.
