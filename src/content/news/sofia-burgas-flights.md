If you're planning your trip to Eurovision 2027, you might find that flying directly into Burgas (BOJ) is either too expensive or doesn't have direct connections from your home city. As a result, tens of thousands of fans are expected to fly into the capital, Sofia (SOF), and make their way to the coast.

While the bus takes about 5-6 hours, there is a much faster alternative: **domestic flights**.

## Bulgaria Air's Regular Schedule
The national carrier, **Bulgaria Air**, has confirmed that it maintains year-round regular flight service between Sofia (SOF) and Burgas (BOJ). 

Here is what you need to know:
*   **Flight Duration:** The flight is incredibly quick, taking just **50 minutes** from takeoff to landing.
*   **Terminal:** Domestic flights to Burgas depart from **Terminal 2** at Sofia Airport.
*   **Frequency:** During the winter schedule, flights operate 4 days a week. However, during the summer season (which includes the Eurovision weeks in May), the schedule expands to almost daily flights.

## Should You Book Now?
Tickets for May 2027 are not fully loaded into the global reservation systems yet, but we highly recommend setting up flight alerts. Given the massive influx of fans, these 50-minute domestic hops will sell out incredibly fast once delegations and press begin securing their logistics.
