Burgas is much more than just the home of Arena Burgas and the Eurovision 2027 Grand Final. It is the pearl of the Bulgarian Black Sea coast, offering a perfect blend of relaxed beach vibes, vibrant culture, and historical depth. If you are planning your trip for May 2027, be sure to add these must-see locations and activities to your itinerary.

## 1. Stroll Through the Sea Garden (Morska Gradina)
Stretching for 5 kilometers along the coastline, the Sea Garden is an immense green oasis and the true heart of Burgas. It’s the perfect place for a morning jog, a scenic picnic, or a relaxing walk before the evening concerts. Don't miss the **Burgas Pier (The Bridge)**, an iconic structure that extends 300 meters into the sea, offering breathtaking sunset views.

## 2. Escape to St. Anastasia Island
Did you know Bulgaria has a mysterious inhabited island in the Black Sea? Passenger boats depart daily from the Port of Burgas (Magazia 1) to St. Anastasia Island. Once a medieval monastery and later a political prison, it has been transformed into a beautiful tourist destination featuring a museum, a boutique hotel, and a traditional restaurant serving freshly caught fish.
*👉 [Reserve your boat tickets to St. Anastasia Island early](https://www.getyourguide.com/burgas-l944/?partner_id=3F441)*

## 3. Experience the Pink Salt Pans (Atanasovsko Lake)
Just a few kilometers north of the city center lies a unique natural spa experience. The Atanasovsko Lake features bright pink pools rich in lye and healing mud, famously known as the "Bulgarian Dead Sea". Applying the black mud is not only a fantastic wellness treatment but also a fun, highly Instagrammable activity—and it is completely free to enter!

## 4. Dive into History with a Day Trip to Nessebar
Do you have a free day between the Semi-Finals? A day trip to the ancient town of Nessebar is an absolute must. Located just a 40-minute drive from Burgas, this UNESCO World Heritage site is situated on a rocky peninsula. You will be mesmerized by the cobblestone streets, the ruins of dozens of Byzantine churches, and the cliffside seafood restaurants overlooking the sea.
*👉 [Book a premium day trip from Burgas to Nessebar](https://www.getyourguide.com/nessebar-l128156/?partner_id=3F441)*

## 5. Visit the Aviation Museum in Sarafovo
Located right next to Burgas Airport (BOJ), this open-air museum allows you to get up close to an impressive collection of authentic Soviet-era aircraft. You can even sit in the pilot's seat of an AN-12! It's a fantastic option for aviation enthusiasts or a great way to kill time before your departure flight.
