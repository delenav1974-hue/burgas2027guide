Getting home after a late rehearsal or a night at the EuroClub is one of the questions fans ask us most. Here is where night transport in Burgas stands today.

## A second night line before Eurovision

Deputy Mayor Stanimir Apostolov has backed the idea of a **second seasonal night bus line**, planned to start in **May 2027**, just before the contest. The proposed route runs from the Izgrev terminal through the city centre to the South and Meden Rudnik terminals. The exact route is still being analysed, and the line would need five additional buses.

## Proposals that are not approved yet

- A seasonal night line linking the South Bus Station, Lazur, Izgrev, Zornitsa, Burgas Airport and Sarafovo.
- A summer night bus from Burgas to Sozopol via Kraimorie and Chernomorets.
- 24, 48 and 72-hour and weekly tourist travel passes.

None of these has been adopted, so please do not plan your trip around them yet.
